# Privacy Policy

## What we collect

- **Name and email**, entered during setup and sent to himudigonda.me.
- **Usage analytics**, tied to a random anonymous ID and sent to
  himudigonda.me: app launches, the voice, speed, and length of generated audio,
  audiobook uploads (page count and file type), listening time, exports, and
  update-installer events. Your text and documents are not included. You can
  turn this off in **Preferences -> General -> Share Usage Analytics**; Voqora
  asks you to confirm.

We use this information to run and improve Voqora and to contact you about the
product. We do not sell it.

## What stays on your Mac

Speech is generated on your Mac. Your documents, generated audio, history,
and audiobook progress are stored locally and are removed when you delete them
or use **Preferences -> Data -> Erase All Local Data**.

The Stats page (listening time, voices, streaks, and library totals) is
computed from a file on your Mac. It is never sent anywhere, you can reset it
from the page, and Erase All Local Data clears it. It is separate from the
usage analytics above.

## Third-party services

- **AI cleanup providers**: AI cleanup is off unless you connect a provider
  and turn it on for a document. For a cloud provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI,
  Groq, Cerebras, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter), the relevant page text and
  images of scanned pages are sent to that provider under its terms, using your
  own key. Ollama and LM Studio run on your Mac and keep that text there. A
  Custom server receives the text at the address you enter, so it may be on
  your Mac, on your network, or remote; Voqora requires https for a remote
  server when a key is set. Your API keys are stored in your macOS login
  Keychain. Voqora sends a key only to its own service on your Mac, with each
  cleanup request, and from there to the provider you connected. Keys are not
  included in analytics.
- **GitHub**: Voqora checks GitHub for new releases and downloads updates from
  GitHub when you choose to.
